Read-only Sprint reports and event timelines for Odoo 19 Community, depending
on `project_scrum`. Metrics come from immutable Sprint scope events, not current
task estimates or stage names. Native lists, forms, pivots and graphs supplement
ordinary Project reporting.

All point measures use story points rounded to two decimal places. Velocity is
completed in-scope points for closed Sprints and is zero before closure.
Burndown is unfinished in-scope points; burnup shows completed and total scope.
Scope Change shows initial, added, removed and final points. Commitment vs
Completion compares original committed points with completed committed points;
its ratio uses fractions rather than percentages.
